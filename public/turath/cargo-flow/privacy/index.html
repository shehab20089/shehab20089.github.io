Privacy policy

Cargo Flow for Android

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The advertising and purchase features described here apply to version 0.3.0 and later. Version 0.2.0 has no ads or in-app purchases.

About the game

Cargo Flow is made by Turath. It has no accounts, and you can play it offline.

Ads and Google's services

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your internet address), and how you interact with the ads.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK also collects app set IDs and other applicable device or account identifiers, app interactions (such as app launches, taps, and video views), and diagnostic information about the app and SDK's performance. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data sent by this SDK is encrypted in transit.

Where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the game asks for consent before loading ads. You can change your choice with Privacy choices in the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The No ads purchase is paid through Google Play. We never see your payment details. It turns off ads between levels permanently. Ads you choose to watch for a reward remain optional.

Progress, settings, and permissions

Your progress and settings are saved only on this device, and uninstalling the game deletes them. The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.
